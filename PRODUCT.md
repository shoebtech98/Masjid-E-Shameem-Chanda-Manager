# Product

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Masjid E Shameem treasurers who record and review household contributions.

## Product Purpose

Chanda Manager helps the treasurer record monthly household chanda and review collection status, payment history, and reports. The top-priority task is recording a household payment quickly. Success means accurate, understandable collection records that are quick to enter and easy to review.

## Operating Context

The treasurer uses the mobile app on a phone while managing household contributions, often entering a payment at the point of collection and checking household or period totals later.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing mobile app provides sign-in, dashboard, collection entry, household management, monthly and annual reports, and account settings. It connects to the organization's existing server; the mobile app does not own a separate database. Preserve existing API behavior and financial safeguards. The app is built with Expo and React Native for iOS and Android.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Masjid E Shameem identity and existing mosque icon. Preserve all existing mobile workflows and financial safeguards. Avoid slowing payment entry, obscuring amounts or payment status, or making financial data feel decorative.

## Evidence on Hand

The working product and source are in `mobile/`. The web app and its product documentation describe household and payment workflows. Do not invent endorsements, impact metrics, or financial claims.

## Product Principles

- Keep contribution records accurate and explicit.
- Make the current collection status easy to understand.
- Make household and payment details retrievable.
- Keep account access and session handling secure on native devices.
- Preserve the existing server as the source of truth.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support native safe areas, accessible labels and roles, touch-friendly controls, system appearance preferences, and readable content at increased text sizes on iOS and Android.
